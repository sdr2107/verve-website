# Moodboard — what to borrow, and what to refuse

Kept 2026-10-20. The five reference screenshots (a concept dashboard called
Neka/Nekta, seen on X; concept art, not a shipping product — the name flips,
the dates flip between 2024 and 2026, one tile says "stops") live in
`docs/inspiration/`, which is gitignored: they are someone else's images and
this repository is public. They are a moodboard, never assets.

## Borrow

1. **The figure with readings pinned to the body.** A person in profile, and
   three or four live readings pointing at where they come from: heart rate
   at the chest, breathing at the throat, temperature at the shoulder. For
   the landing hero, built from Verve's own figures and example values that
   are marked as examples. The reading says the number and one plain word
   ("48 bpm · your usual"), never a grade.
2. **The sleep timeline with event markers.** One bar for the night with the
   moments named under it: fell asleep 22:37, deep sleep peak 03:24, woke
   07:58, in bed 9h 21. The app's Sleep page already has the bar; the named
   moments are the borrowing.
3. **Insights as one-line sentences.** "You fell asleep 18 minutes faster
   than usual." The Guide already writes this way; the site should show it
   that way, one sentence a line, no icon doing the work the sentence does.
4. **The reflection.** A reader suggested a mirrored figure. Keep the image,
   not the gesture: today's you facing your 30-night usual self is exactly
   how every Verve number is read. No kiss on a goal met; a reward animation
   is a different product.

## Refuse

- Composite scores: Sleep Score 84, Nutrition score 82, Body Battery 78,
  Strain 0.5, Recovery 84%. Verve has never built one and the refusal is a
  selling point (see the intrinsic-capacity memory: no composite, ever).
- Heart Age 25, "−6 years". A claim the evidence does not support at the
  precision shown.
- Population bands presented as the reading ("Normal 45–65"). Verve reads a
  person against their own months; the population figure is context, cited.
- Gradient tiles with a different pastel per metric. Verve's palette is one
  accent and semantic colour by state.

## Where it goes

The landing hero (borrow 1) is the next website design task after the app's
weekly-dose card; the sleep moments (borrow 2) are an app change on the Sleep
page. Neither is started.
