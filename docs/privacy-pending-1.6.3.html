Verve — Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 6, 2026 · Last updated: October 26, 2026

Verve is a fitness and health tracker made by Sadiya Healthcare Private Limited, a company registered in India (CIN U58202MR2026PTC482242) with its registered office at Flat 403, Plot 152, Sector 2, Ulwe, Panvel, Raigad, Maharashtra 410206, India ("we"). It is an iPhone app ("the App") and a website, verve-app.health ("the Website"), which share one account. This policy explains what Verve collects, where it is kept, who else receives it, and what you can do about it. It covers both the App and the Website.

In brief
  • You need an account to use Verve. While you are signed in, what you enter and the workouts and weight Verve reads from Apple Health are backed up to our servers in Mumbai, India.
  • Daily watch readings (sleep, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, steps and more) are backed up only if you turn on Back up watch readings. It is off until you turn it on.
  • When you ask the Guide (Verve's AI feature) or upload a report to be read, that information is sent to Anthropic, the AI provider, to produce the answer. Your name and email are not sent with Guide requests.
  • On iPhone, Verve asks before using your advertising identifier. Our attribution partner Tenjin and our subscription service RevenueCat receive device and install information either way.
  • We do not sell your data, and nothing read from Apple Health is used for advertising.

1. What Verve collects

Your account

Your email address and password, or your Apple or Google sign-in. With Sign in with Apple, Apple may give us your name and email (or a private relay address).

The first time your account is opened on a phone it has not been on before, we email you once to say so. The email names the time, the kind of phone (iPhone or Android), its system version and the Verve version; Verve does not collect your location, so it names no place. It is a security notice, so it has no unsubscribe.

What you enter

  • Profile: name, age, sex, height, weight, resting heart rate, waist, ethnicity, smoking history, and whether you take beta-blockers.
  • Health questions: your answers to the exercise-readiness questions, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, family history of heart disease, and whether a doctor has cleared you to exercise.
  • Workouts and fitness tests: workouts you log (type, time, duration, effort, notes) and the results of fitness tests.
  • Lab and body values: blood pressure, glucose, HbA1c, insulin, cholesterol, apolipoproteins and other blood tests, whether you take medicine for diabetes, blood pressure or cholesterol, body fat and muscle measurements, grip strength and other strength and balance tests, waist and hip.
  • Preferences: your weekly goal, units and display settings.
  • Questions you ask the Guide.

What Verve reads from Apple Health or Health Connect (with your permission)

Workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, heart rate recovery, VO2 max, sleep, respiratory rate, steps, walking and running distance, stand time, active and resting energy, blood pressure, weight, height, body fat percentage, lean body mass, waist circumference, date of birth and biological sex. Verve only reads; it never writes to Apple Health. On Android, Verve reads from Health Connect instead: exercise sessions, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, VO2 max, steps, distance, active and basal energy, sleep, respiratory rate, blood pressure, weight, height, body fat and lean body mass. It reads only while the App is open, never in the background, and it never writes to Health Connect.

Device and install information

When the App starts, our attribution partner (Tenjin) and our subscription service (RevenueCat) receive information about your device and how the App was installed. Section 5 explains exactly what. If the App crashes, a report of the crash goes to Sentry (section 6); it carries no account or health data. Verve does not ask for or use your device's location.

2. Where your data is kept

On your phone

Everything Verve shows you is stored on your phone, protected by the phone's own encryption (iOS or Android). Your sign-in token is kept in the iOS Keychain or, on Android, in storage protected by the Android Keystore.

Your account backup

While you are signed in, Verve backs up the following to your account on Supabase, on servers in Mumbai, India. It is sent over an encrypted connection (TLS), encrypted at rest, and protected so that only your account can read it. Signing in is required to use the App, and this backup has no separate switch; it is what the account is for. It lets the Website show your data and brings it back if you reinstall or change phones.

  • Everything listed under "What you enter" above, except your Guide questions, which are not stored.
  • The workouts Verve reads from Apple Health or Health Connect, with their heart rate, calories and effort.
  • Your weight history: the last 400 days of weighings from Apple Health or Health Connect, and any weight you type.

Daily watch readings: only if you turn them on

Resting heart rate, steps, sleep times and stages, one-minute heart rate recovery, night heart rate variability, energy burned, standing and sitting time, and walking cadence are backed up only if you turn on Back up watch readings in Settings → Your data → Backup. It is off until you turn it on. The first backup after you turn it on covers up to five years of these daily readings, then the last five weeks on each sync. Turning it off deletes the backed-up readings from your account at once.

Blood pressure read from Apple Health or Health Connect is shown in the App but not backed up. Blood pressure you type is backed up with your other values.

3. The Guide (AI)

The Guide compares two periods of your activity and explains what changed. It runs only when you tap it or ask it a question; free accounts get one reading a week. Each time, our server sends the following to Anthropic's Claude model to write the answer:

  • your age and sex, your latest fitness test result and your weekly goal;
  • what changed between the two periods in your activity, such as training minutes, MET-hours, steps, Zone 2 and strength days;
  • your body markers that are outside their healthy range, with their values (for example blood pressure, glucose or waist), and how many are in range; or, when comparing years, the markers measured in both years;
  • summaries of your sleep, night heart rate variability, resting heart rate, heart rate recovery and sitting time. These come from Apple Health on your phone, so they are sent whether or not Back up watch readings is on;
  • the question you type, word for word.

Your name, email and account details are not sent. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, requests are not used to train models and are deleted from Anthropic's systems within 30 days, unless kept to enforce its usage policy or as required by law. We keep only a count of how many requests your account made each day, to apply the free limit. If you don't want this information sent, don't use the Guide; nothing is sent until you do.

Remembering its reads. The first time you open a Guide read, the App asks whether the Guide may remember what it told you. If you say yes, we keep each read and the figures of the period it judged (dose, training minutes, Zone 2 minutes, resting heart rate, night HRV) in your account, so later reads can say whether the advice held. Nothing else is stored; what happened next is worked out from your weeks at the time of the next read. It is off until you say yes; turn it off in Settings → Your data → Backup and the kept reads are deleted; they are deleted with your account.

4. The Website and My health

Signing in

The Website uses the same account as the App. You sign in with a password or with a code we email you. The name you give at the greeting is stored in your profile and used in the App.

Reports you upload

PDFs and photos of your lab and imaging reports are stored in a private folder that only your account can open, on Supabase servers in Mumbai, India. Photos are reduced in size in your browser before upload. Deleting a report removes the file at once.

Reading a report

To read a report, our server sends the whole file to Anthropic's Claude model. That means everything printed on it reaches Anthropic, including the patient's name, date of birth and any ID numbers on the page. The model returns the values, units, reference ranges, dates, the report's wording, and the patient name as printed, which we use to check the report belongs to the right person. Under Anthropic's commercial terms the file and the reply are not used to train models and are deleted from Anthropic's systems within 30 days, unless kept to enforce its usage policy or as required by law. We count how many reports each account reads per day, to apply a limit.

What Verve keeps from a report

Every value read, with its unit, the lab's printed reference range, its section and date; an imaging report's impression and findings as printed; the report's type, date and the patient name as printed; and, for the markers the App tracks, a copy in your App's lab history, marked unchecked until you confirm it there. These stay after the file is deleted. Email us to have particular values removed, or delete your account to remove everything.

People you look after

You can keep pages for people you look after, such as a parent or a child, with their name, birth year and reports. Only do this if you are their parent or guardian or have their permission. You are responsible for what you add about them, and you can remove it at any time.

Sharing your page

You can give someone, such as a family member, coach or clinician, access to parts of your My health page by entering their email address and choosing what they may see (labs, imaging, activity, body, heart, notes). We store their email, the scopes you chose, both your names, and when they last viewed it. We email them an invitation; if they don't have a Verve account, one is created for that email address so they can accept. The email names who invited them and which parts are shared; it contains none of your health data. You can also ask someone to share their page with you, which stores the same details. You can stop sharing at any time.

Notes by a coach or clinician

A coach or clinician you invite may keep a note on your page. The note is theirs, stored on our Supabase servers in Mumbai, readable only by them and only while your share is active, and they can export it at any time. When you stop sharing, the note is no longer readable on Verve. For the coaches and clinicians we have vetted, we also record their email address, their role, a short title and the date we checked them, so that your Give access panel can show a verified badge.

Messages and files with a coach or clinician

If you share your page with a coach or clinician, you and they can send each other messages and files (PDFs or images up to 15 MB, for example a training plan), and pin the plan you are working to. Each message is dated, shows who sent it, and shows when it was read. Messages and files are stored on our Supabase servers in Mumbai, the files in a private store opened only through a short-lived link.

Either of you can write only while your share is active, and no one can change a message after it is sent. The thread belongs to you: if you stop sharing, you keep every message and file, and the coach or clinician can no longer see them (they keep only what they exported). Messages and files are deleted when you delete your account or the page they belong to. Verve does not read, use or analyse them, and they are never sent to an AI provider. Messages are not for emergencies.

Weekly report

You can choose to send a coach or clinician an automatic weekly report on the day you pick (Monday unless you change it). It contains last week's figures as the app counts them: your activity dose against your goal, minutes of moderate to vigorous activity, strength days, average steps, resting heart rate and any fitness test, and only the parts your share allows. It is written by our database from your numbers, not by an AI. You can turn it off at any time, and it stops when the share stops.

The Guide’s read on the Website

On the In depth part of My health you can ask for a short written read of your page. It is off until you turn it on, and it runs only when you ask for it.

When you ask, our server sends the numbers the page has already worked out to Anthropic's Claude model to write the read: your age band and sex, and computed figures such as your fitness test results, weekly activity, sleep and heart readings, and which lab values sit inside or outside each report's own printed range. Your name, your email address, your report files, the wording of your reports and any notes are never sent. If you look at someone else's page through a share, only what their share covers is sent, and only after we check you are allowed to see it.

Under Anthropic's commercial terms the request and the reply are not used to train models and are deleted from Anthropic's systems within 30 days, unless retained for legal or safety reasons. We keep the written read on our Supabase servers in Mumbai so the page does not ask again until your numbers change; it is deleted when you delete your account. The read is wellness information, not a diagnosis or medical advice.

Each read is filed on your page by date, with any note you add, so you can compare reads and download or share one. Reads are kept until you delete them or your account. A read you share with a coach or clinician is copied into your Messages with them. Your first read is free; further reads come with Pro.

Newsletter

You can ask for the newsletter in three places: the box on the sign-up form, the question on My health, and the email box on the Start page. In each case we record the address, the time, and the sentence you agreed to, on our Supabase servers in Mumbai, India. A welcome email is sent through Resend. The newsletter itself is sent through Substack, which holds your address under its own privacy policy once we move it there. We use the address for the newsletter and for nothing else. Every letter carries an unsubscribe link, and a reply asking to stop is honoured: the address is then removed from our list and from Substack.

On your device

The Website sets no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising scripts. Your browser keeps your sign-in session and a few small preferences until you sign out or clear site data. One page, the Start page's sheet, loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when you open it. The Website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security; Verve does not receive them.

5. Advertising attribution and subscriptions

Tenjin (ad attribution)

We use Tenjin to learn which advertisements bring people to the App. On iPhone, the App first asks your permission through Apple's tracking prompt, and Tenjin starts only after you answer, either way.

  • If you allow tracking, Tenjin receives your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Either way, Tenjin receives the vendor identifier, your IP address, basic device details, install and session information, and Apple's SKAdNetwork install reports.
  • Once you sign in, Tenjin is told your Verve account ID, so that sign-ups and subscriptions can be matched to the install.
  • Tenjin receives four events: sign-up, trial start, subscription, and a completed fitness test. For the fitness test it learns only that one was recorded, not the result. No health or fitness values are sent to Tenjin.

On Android there is no tracking prompt. Tenjin receives the same install information, including Google Play's install referrer and your advertising ID if your device provides one.

RevenueCat (subscriptions)

RevenueCat manages subscriptions and free trials. It receives your Verve account ID, your purchase and subscription status from the App Store, your device identifiers (the advertising identifier only if you allowed tracking), Apple's ad-attribution token, and Tenjin's installation ID, so that subscription events can be matched to the install. No health or fitness data is sent to RevenueCat.

Apple and Google

Payments are handled by the App Store; Verve never sees your card. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, they handle your sign-in under their own privacy policies.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Verve does not collect a push-notification token.

7. How long we keep it, and deleting it

  • On your phone: until you clear it in Settings or uninstall the App.
  • Your account backup: while your account exists. When you delete a workout or an entry, it is hidden everywhere and marked deleted in your backup; it is erased when you delete your account.
  • Daily watch readings: until you turn off Back up watch readings or delete your account.
  • Uploaded reports: until you delete them. The values read from them stay while your account exists.
  • AI requests and report files sent for reading: deleted by Anthropic within 30 days.
  • Deleting your account (App → Settings → Delete account, or Delete my account on the Website's My health page) deletes, at once and without you having to contact us: your account, your backup, your uploaded reports and every value read from them, the people you look after and everything filed under them, your shares and notes, your newsletter sign-up, and your customer record at RevenueCat.
  • What deleting your account cannot reach: data still on your phone, until you delete the App; your address at Substack if it was already moved there (use the unsubscribe link); Tenjin's install records, kept under Tenjin's own policy (email us and we will ask Tenjin to delete them); a RevenueCat record of a phone from before you signed in on it, holding device identifiers only, which RevenueCat keeps apart from your account's record (email us and we will ask RevenueCat to delete it); and what Apple and Google hold under their own policies.

8. Your choices and rights

  • See and export your data: Settings → Export & erase, as JSON or CSV.
  • Clear the data on your phone: Settings → Export & erase.
  • Turn watch-reading backup on or off: Settings → Your data → Backup.
  • Change Apple Health access: iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Verve.
  • Change Health Connect access: Android Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → Verve.
  • Change tracking permission: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • View or delete a report: the Reports tab on the Website.
  • Stop sharing your page: the Give access panel on My health.
  • Unsubscribe from the newsletter: the link in any letter.
  • Delete your account: App → Settings → Delete account, or Delete my account on My health. Everything on our servers is deleted at once.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct something, to delete particular data, or to withdraw your consent. Email the address below; we reply within 30 days.

9. Security

  • All connections use HTTPS/TLS.
  • Data on our servers is encrypted at rest and protected by row-level security, so each account can read only its own data and what has been shared with it.
  • Your sign-in token is kept in the iOS Keychain or, on Android, in Keystore-protected storage.
  • If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Children

Verve is for adults aged 18 and over. Don't create an account if you are under 18. If you add a page for a child you look after (section 4), you must be their parent or guardian. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

11. Apple Health and Health Connect

  • We do not use Apple Health or Health Connect data for advertising or marketing, and we do not sell it.
  • No health or fitness data, from Apple Health, Health Connect or entered by you, is sent to Tenjin or RevenueCat.
  • Apple Health and Health Connect data is shared with a third party only to give you a Verve feature you asked for: summaries go to Anthropic when you use the Guide (section 3).
  • Workouts and weight read from Apple Health or Health Connect, and daily watch readings if you turn the switch on, are backed up to your account on Supabase as described in section 2. Verve never stores health-store data in iCloud or Google Drive.
