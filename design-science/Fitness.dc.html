METs vs MET-hours

One is how hard, the other is how much

The difference in one line

A MET is intensity: one MET is you at rest, four METs is a brisk walk. A MET-hour is volume: four METs held for half an hour is two MET-hours. Verve tracks both, and never adds them together.

Figures in METs. Your own table depends on your age and sex, and on which dataset covers you: the Cleveland Clinic's treadmill data from 40, the Cooper Institute's norms below it. There is no middle band on purpose: the boundary between below average and above average is the median of the people in the study, so everyone falls on one side of it.

Being below average rather than above it carried a hazard ratio of 1.41 in 122,007 patients, the same order of risk as smoking or diabetes. From the lowest tier to the highest the difference was fivefold, and the benefit was larger for women in every tier. The elite band has no ceiling: in 750,000 veterans followed for a decade, risk kept falling at the top.

Mandsager et al, JAMA Network Open 2018

Test your CRF

Which test you are offered, and why it might not be the hard one

In the app

A short health screen runs before your first test. A beta-blocker, or a resting pressure at or above 180 over 110, and the six-minute walk is the only test offered. Cleared and training regularly, and the twelve-minute run is recommended instead. You can always pick another.

6-minute walksubmaximal
12-minute runmaximal
blocked at 180/110 or above
retest nudged at 8 weeks

Eight weeks is not a round number. Aerobic adaptation has a half-time of ten to eleven days and a reliable change needs on the order of twenty-one sessions, which puts an honest retest at six to nine weeks. Testing sooner is not wrong; it is just unlikely to show much, and Verve says so rather than discouraging you.

How accurate each test is

What the stars mean, and where each test stops working

Lab test, directmeasuredGas analysis. The only one that measures rather than estimates.
12-minute runr ≈ 0.90Strong, if you truly go all out. Pacing conservatively under-reads you.
6-minute walksubmaximalBuilt for lower fitness. It under-reads a fit person by design.
Watch estimate7 to 16 % errorCarried with every wearable reading Verve shows. Never the headline.

A correlation of 0.90 is strong and is not the same as agreement. It means the test ranks people well, not that your number is within a beat of the truth. Verve keeps test types apart for the same reason: a walk test and a maximal run measure differently, so a trend across them would show the change of method, not a change in you.

The run protocols

What the app's checklist is short for

The twelve-minute run wants a flat measured route, a warm-up, and an even effort you can only just hold to the end. The app's checklist is the short version; the full protocol, including what to do if you have to stop, is written out here. Chest pain, dizziness, nausea or breathlessness beyond hard breathing end the test, and the result does not matter.
